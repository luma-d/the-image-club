01 — About

The Image Club is a photography club for film and digital photographers in Damascus. We develop, print, and share our work together — and we are opening soon.

02 — Workshops

In September 2026 we ran our first workshops in Damascus.

01

Black & White Film Developing

Develop your own rolls: chemistry, tanks, timing, and drying prints in a working darkroom.

02

C-41 Colour Film Developing

Develop colour negative film at home — chemistry, temperature control, and scanning-ready results.

03

Cyanotype Printing

Sun-printed camera negatives and objects on coated paper, the oldest of the photographic processes.

New workshops and open darkroom sessions are announced first on Instagram.
